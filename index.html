Why Happy Brew exists

13%

Only 13% of individuals with intellectual disabilities in Florida are employed.

People with intellectual and developmental disabilities are too often overlooked, and left without meaningful work or connection.

Many of us want that to change but don’t know how to help. That’s where a cup of coffee comes in.

What we believe

Everyone deserves to be seen, valued and included.

Happy Brew provides employment, job training and leadership opportunities for individuals with intellectual and developmental differences, in a community café where hope is shared. Abilities and friendships grow here one cup of coffee and conversation at a time.

  • More than 25 Brew Crew members with intellectual and developmental disabilities work here
  • A 15-week internship program led by a licensed occupational therapist
  • A café designed to go beyond ADA standards
  • A 501(c)(3) nonprofit with Candid’s Silver Transparency seal (2025)
