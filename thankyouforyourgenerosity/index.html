Thank you

Thank you for your generosity.

Your gift helps create meaningful employment, job training and belonging for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities through the Happy Brew community café.

Because of your generosity, opportunities are being created, confidence is being built, and hope is being shared every day.

If you’d like to stay connected, check out our upcoming events, stop by the café, or follow along as we continue brewing possibilities together.

From all of us at Happy Brew, thank you for being part of this community.
