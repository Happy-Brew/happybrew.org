Common questions

How do I book a meeting room at Happy Brew?

Send us a request using the form below. Our team will follow up to confirm availability and finalize your booking. We respond to rental requests within 24 hours.

How much does it cost to rent a meeting room?

Nonprofit organizations rent at $25 an hour. Companies and other organizations rent at $50 an hour. Add-ons like coffee service ($25) and pastry platters ($50) are available at booking.

How many people can the meeting rooms hold?

Each of our two conference rooms comfortably seats 12 to 20 people. If your group is larger, ask about our full venue rental options.

Is there a minimum booking time?

Please reach out through the request form for current minimum booking requirements. We’re happy to work with your schedule.

Can we get food and coffee in our meeting room?

Yes. Fresh drip coffee service can be added to your meeting space for $25. Assorted pastry platters are available for $50. Your team can also set up a house account to order at the counter directly. For larger catering needs, we partner with Pine Castle for full catering options.

Is the meeting room private?

Yes. Both conference rooms are private, enclosed spaces separate from the main coffee shop floor, so your team can meet without interruption.

Do you offer a discount for nonprofits?

We do, and we’re proud of it. Nonprofits rent at $25 an hour as part of our commitment to supporting organizations doing good work in Jacksonville.

Can I tour the meeting rooms before booking?

Absolutely. We’d love to show you the space. Send a request below or stop by during business hours.

What if I need to cancel?

Please let us know 24 hours in advance if you need to cancel, so we may rent the space to another group. Otherwise you will be charged.
