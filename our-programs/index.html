Internship program

Fifteen weeks to build confidence for work

Happy Brew offers a unique internship program that is different from our employment opportunities.

Our space hosts a weekly professional development group facilitated by a licensed occupational therapist. This 15-week program is specifically designed for individuals with disabilities. It aims to build confidence and workplace skills in a supportive and empowering environment.

  • Learn in a professional setting that encourages collaboration and group discussion
  • Interns are chosen on a first come, first served basis, and the group grows as the program grows

Questions about the internship or pricing?

Reach out to Sally MacKay, our licensed occupational therapist.

sally@happybrew.org
