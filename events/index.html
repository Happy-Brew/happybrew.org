Events

Something’s always brewing.

We want to use Happy Brew as a tool to build community. Come run, paint, shop, or just hang out. Everyone and every ability is welcome.

Coming up

All events are at Happy Brew, 3200 Hendricks Ave. Follow @happybrewjax on Instagram for the latest.

Nov27Fri

beFriend Gathering, presented by 99 Balloons

Friday, November 27, 2026 · 6:00 to 8:00 pm

beFriend is a place where adults with and without disabilities gather with the sole purpose of building community, establishing new friendships, and hanging out as adult friends. Join us!

Happens monthly, usually on the fourth Friday. Learn more at 99balloons.org

Nov28Sat

Local Pop Up Market

Saturday, November 28, 2026 · 10:00 am to 1:00 pm

Join us at our monthly neighborhood pop up market featuring art and artisanal goods made by creators of ALL ABILITIES!

Happens monthly, usually on the fourth Saturday.

Dec5Sat

Happy Brew Art Club

Saturday, December 5, 2026 · 1:00 to 3:00 pm

Creativity has no limits. Everyone and every ability is welcome! Registration is required.

Happens monthly, usually on the first Saturday.

Sign up for Art Club

Dec12Sat

Happy Brew Run Club

Saturday, December 12, 2026 · 8:30 to 11:00 am (come early)

Come get in some Happy Miles! There’s both a 2 mile and a 5K loop, open to all abilities. You can run, you can walk, or you can wheel.

Brew Buddies guide the way for anyone who may need a little extra help. Ainsley’s Angels will be there with their special running chairs, so if you want to take part but can’t walk, run or wheel yourself, you can be pushed by an Angel!

Happens monthly, usually on the second Saturday. You can find us on Strava too.
