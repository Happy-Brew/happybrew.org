Our story

We brew possABILITIES.

Happy Brew’s mission is to provide meaningful employment, job training and belonging for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities, in a community café where hope is shared.

What we believe

Five things we hold on to

  • Coffee

    We believe a great cup of coffee is the start to great conversations.

  • Community

    We believe community can collapse barriers and change the world! We want to be a place where everyone feels welcome.

  • Compassion

    We believe every person has worth and should be valued. We advocate for individuals with intellectual and developmental differences.

  • Collaboration

    We believe partnerships are key. We partner with Southside Methodist Church and local organizations to share the joy of community and the Good News of Jesus Christ.

  • Creativity

    We believe showing the world what’s possible through inclusive design brings hope and creates opportunities.

How it started

An idea that brewed out of listening

The Happy Brew café idea brewed out of Southside students’ involvement in the Missing Voices Project through Flagler College. As we listened to families, we learned that students with intellectual and developmental differences (IDD) could teach us more about love and grace than we could ever discover on our own.

We wanted to create a place where we could be in community together. What better place than a café designed for all abilities?

Thirty years of friendship first

The Friends Ministry at Southside Methodist has existed for over 30 years to transform the lives of individuals with intellectual and developmental differences, their families and volunteers, by creating opportunities for belonging, contributing and celebrating God-given gifts.

That ministry has grown with a Young Life Capernaum club, which reaches students with IDD and gives them a place to gather as they age forward.

Why a coffee shop?

Historically, the majority of people with IDD have been either unemployed or underemployed, despite their ability, desire and willingness to work in the community. People with IDD can work in the community alongside people without disabilities and earn competitive wages. Knowing that, the idea for a coffee shop was born.

Our goal is to support graduating high schoolers with IDD, who face the reality of an 80% unemployment rate. We want to give these young adults opportunities to grow, lead, work and use their abilities alongside their neurotypical peers, in a café where our community can come together in shared hope.

Happy Brew is a ministry first and a business second.

The road so far

  1. Summer 2019Southside Methodist Church joins the Missing Voices Project. A team of high schoolers and adults starts dreaming about giving people with IDD a voice and job opportunities.
  2. Fall 2023Happy Brew opens its automatic doors at 3200 Hendricks Ave.
  3. October 2025We celebrate two years, with more than 25 Brew Crew members with intellectual and developmental disabilities employed.
  4. March 2026Our outdoor stage opens with a ribbon cutting, Run Club and a full day of live music.
