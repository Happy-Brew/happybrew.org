Our story

Built so everyone can walk, roll or stroll right in.

Our goal at Happy Brew is to go beyond the minimum ADA standards. We want an inclusive space our whole community can enjoy: wheelchair users, older adults, people who are deaf or blind, children, and many others.

What you’ll find here

Designed for all abilities

By creating a safe space, we hope to promote social interaction and community building.

  • Ample wheelchair-accessible parking
  • A speed table crosswalk connecting accessible parking to the door
  • No-step entry with wide, automatic doors
  • Tables of different heights, with adjustable table options
  • A sensory corner with headphones and soothing activities
  • Four large ADA bathrooms and an adult-sized changing table
  • A staff trained to accommodate your individual needs

The idea

What is universal design?

It’s the difference between a ramp around the back and one front door that everyone uses together.

Read more about universal design from the IDEA Center

Help us do better

The best ideas come from you

We developed our plans by listening to people in our community and consulting experts. We still believe the best ideas come from you.

If there are ideas or changes you would like to see, please tell us how we can make sure you and your loved ones always feel comfortable at Happy Brew.
