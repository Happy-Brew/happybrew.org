Our story

Meet the Happy Brew crew

Behind every cup is an inter-ability team, and behind that team are the staff and volunteer board who keep the mission brewing.

Staff

  • Amy Franks

    CEO / President

    The chief coffee sipper. Amy attended undergrad at the University of Alabama and received her Masters in Health Administration at UNF. She has over 15 years of experience in church ministry and has served in BSF Leadership, Women’s Ministry, and is the former Youth Director at SUMC.

  • Courtenay Bowser

    Manager

    Courtenay started her first not-for-profit at the age of 19: a center for the arts called Shoshannah Arts, offering classes in dance, theatre, music and visual art to students of all ages and abilities. She was the Executive and Artistic Director for almost ten years. She and her husband Jeremiah then went on to found Ignition Point Ministries, which has led them to nations all over the world for almost 20 years, speaking and using music and the arts. Courtenay fell in love with Happy Brew when her son Jed became an assistant manager in the very beginning, and she is so excited to be helping lead the Happy Brew team as manager!

  • Sally MacKay

    Licensed Occupational Therapist

    Sally is a licensed occupational therapist with a heart for working with individuals with developmental disabilities. Her passion began while at the University of Florida, working with the Inclusive Fitness and Unified Sports Program. She graduated with her master’s from Florida Gulf Coast in 2019 and has been working for Brooks Rehabilitation Hospital since graduation.

Board of directors

  • Tom Rossi

    Board Officer

    Tom has 20 years of experience in leading software development companies. He graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Florida. He was the founder and president of Capernaum, Inc, a successful software company that specialized in web application development. He then co-founded Molehill, LLC., StreamCare, and Higher Pixels.

  • Bob Joel

    Board Officer

    Robert Joel, CFP®, is the co-founder of Salvus Wealth Management and serves as Chief Investment Officer and Chief Compliance Officer of the firm. He holds a BA in History from Florida State University and was awarded the Certified Financial Planner designation in 1996. He is active in the local non-profit community and currently serves as Chairman of the Board of Directors for Youth Crisis Center Properties and Amplify, Inc.

  • Mara Webb

    Board Officer

    Mara is a marketing and creative strategist. She is a consultant for multiple nonprofit organizations, including serving as Communications Director for SMC, and serves as the President of Broad Street Children’s Foundation. She graduated with a B.A. in Business Administration and Management from Salem College.

  • Christy Iglesias Adams

    Board Member

    Christy is an experienced special education teacher. Driven by her passion to find employment for adults with IDD, she takes pride in providing instruction that focuses on social and vocational skills. She is currently the Project Search Instructor at North Florida School of Special Education and the Friends Ministry Director at SUMC.

  • Debbie Johnson

    Board Member

    Debbie is a Certified Public Accountant and has more than thirty-five years of accounting and managerial experience. In addition, she has more than thirty years of dedicated community service to numerous nonprofits and organizations, assisting in the areas of finance, governance, operations, leadership and strategic planning. She holds a Bachelor of Business Administration and a Master of Business Administration from the University of North Florida. She currently serves on the boards of The Arc Jacksonville, Groundwork Jacksonville, and Slough Ministries International. At her alma mater, she serves on its Foundation Board and Student Affairs Community Council. She is a member of Leadership Jacksonville’s Class of 2011.

  • John Franks

    Board Member

    John has worked in healthcare for over 25 years and was previously the EVP, Ambulatory Enterprise, at Flagler Health+. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Finance from Florida State University and a Master of Health Administration from the University of North Florida.

  • Mark Johnson

    Board Member

    Mark is the President and CEO of Florida Capital Bank. He is also the past Chairman of the Mortgage Bankers of America Secondary Capital Markets Committee, a past member of the Fannie Mae Southeast Region and Residential Funding Corporation Advisory Boards, and a graduate of the School of Mortgage Banking. He holds a Bachelor of Business Administration degree from Valdosta State College.

  • Mark Thiele

    Board Member

    Mark is a lay member of SMC and serves on various committees. He also volunteers for many not-for-profit and professional organizations. He is a licensed architect and holds an NCARB five-year bachelor’s degree of architecture from the University of Miami. Mark is currently the unit head of architecture and design services for Mayo Clinic Florida.

  • Meghan Woods

    Board Member

    Meghan is a mental health counselor at Elite DNA Behavioral Health. She completed her internships at The Arc Jacksonville working with individuals and groups living with IDD. She is a Peer Mentor through the Christopher & Dana Reeve Foundation and Brooks Rehabilitation, where she encourages and supports individuals transitioning and adjusting to life with an acquired disability. Having walked through most of her life with a disability from a traumatic car accident, Meghan is passionate about helping and advocating for people with disabilities.
